# Growth Audit — iFont

**Live:** https://dkobzar-12345.github.io/ifont-flow-rebuild/
**Prototype on a phone:** https://dkobzar-12345.github.io/ifont-flow-rebuild/?open=proto

Five ranked ideas for iFont's onboarding and paywall, each rebuilt as a working screen in iFont's own design. Only the sequence changes — the art direction does not.

| # | Idea | Screen | Potential outcome |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 01 | Give one second chance on close — the $19.99 yearly plan from your App Store listing | Offer on close | ARPU +10–15% |
| 02 | Name their apps on the paywall | Paywall | CR +15–20% |
| 03 | Ask for notifications and "how did you hear" after the paywall | Welcome · How you heard · Notifications | CR +10–15% |
| 04 | Show their own words in your fonts before the price | Loader · Preview | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Put your 4.6 from 14K ratings on the paywall | Paywall | CR +10–15% |

**Combined potential:** CR to purchase up to +25%, ARPU up to +15%.

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for iFont. Every price, rating and feature line comes from iFont's own App Store listing (US storefront) and in-app screens. Preview typefaces are Google Fonts (OFL), self-hosted in `fonts/`.
